# EPCOT World Showcase - Non-Dining, Non-Entertainment Inventory
**Compiled: 2026-09-30.** Scope: the eleven World Showcase country pavilions only. World Celebration, World Discovery, World Nature and festival booths excluded. Live entertainment (musicians, street performers, character greetings other than dedicated walkthrough spaces), restaurants and food carts excluded.

Confidence is marked per line. "CONFIRMED" = official disneyworld.disney.go.com page or two independent sources agreeing. "UNCONFIRMED" = single secondary source only.

---

## 1. MEXICO

### Attractions (rides)
- **Gran Fiesta Tour Starring The Three Caballeros** - indoor boat dark ride (Arrow Dynamics, 15 riders/boat) - **duration DISPUTED: ~7-8 min** (TouringPlans blog) vs "a little over 5 min" (AllEars). Official Disney page states no duration. Operating.

### Films / theater shows
- None.

### Exhibits / galleries / walkthroughs
- **"Animales Fantásticos: Spirited Creatures of Mexico"** - folk-art / alebrije display in the entrance corridor of the Mesoamerican pyramid, walked through on the way in. UNCONFIRMED current status: no official Disney attraction page exists for it and it is not in the official attraction directory. Existence documented by Wikipedia; a 2026 kids-guide source refers to a "Mexico Folk Art Gallery" in the same position.

### Shops
Permanent indoor: **3**
- Plaza de los Amigos - indoor covered open-air marketplace under the pavilion's perpetual-night sky (CONFIRMED, official)
- La Tienda Encantada - indoor (CONFIRMED, official)
- La Princesa de Cristal - indoor (CONFIRMED, official)

Outdoor carts / kiosks: **1**
- El Ranchito del Norte - outdoor kiosk-style shop near the pyramid exterior (CONFIRMED present on official directory; AllEars listed it closed in 2023, so it has since reopened)

CLOSED / flag:
- **Oaxacan wood-carving cart - CLOSED 31 August 2026** (guide4wdw, Sept 2026). Sold hand-carved, hand-painted Oaxacan Valley carvings.

Counting caveat: La Tienda Encantada and La Princesa de Cristal are adjoining rooms off the Plaza de los Amigos marketplace, so some sources count the whole interior as one shop.

### Kids activities
- **Kidcot Fun Stop** - Mexico (CONFIRMED, official; sited near the entrance art gallery)

## 3. CHINA

### Attractions (rides)
- None.

### Films / theater shows
- **Reflections of China** - Circle-Vision 360 degree film, Temple of Heaven theater (standing-only circular theater) - **12 min 30 sec** (CONFIRMED, Wikipedia). **OPERATING.**
  - **CRITICAL STATUS NOTE: "Wondrous China" has NEVER OPENED.** Announced in 2019 as the replacement film (originally for a 2021 debut tied to WDW's 50th), it was shelved indefinitely after COVID and as of July 2026 remains announced-but-unopened. Reflections of China continues to run in the same theater. Do not list Wondrous China as an offering.

### Exhibits / galleries / walkthroughs
- **House of the Whispering Willows Gallery** - exhibition of exclusive Shanghai Disney Resort artwork: drawings, photos and design elements from the resort's attractions, lands, hotels and entertainment district, showing how Imagineers wove Chinese cultural elements into the design. **OPEN** (CONFIRMED, official; hours 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM).

### Shops
Permanent indoor: **1**
- House of Good Fortune - indoor (housewares, tea sets, wall prints, silk robes, porcelain) (CONFIRMED, Wikipedia + AllEars)

Outdoor carts / kiosks: **1**
- Good Fortune Gifts - outdoor kiosk (parasols, puppets, toys, hats) (UNCONFIRMED status; Wikipedia + AllEars 2023)

Data gap: the official Disney shops directory returned **no** China shops at all, which is a directory-completeness failure rather than evidence of closure (see Gaps).

### Kids activities
- **Kidcot Fun Stop** - China (CONFIRMED, official)
- Gardens and a marionette/puppet play spot are cited by a kids-touring source (UNCONFIRMED as a formal facility).

### Other flags
- Lotus Blossom Café's right-side dining room closed 12 August 2026 with no explanation given. **Dining, out of scope.** Reflections of China, the gallery and the shops are NOT affected.

## 6. THE AMERICAN ADVENTURE

### Attractions (rides)
- None.

### Films / theater shows
- **The American Adventure** - Audio-Animatronic stage show with rear-projection film, Liberty Theater (capacity 1,024 per show) - **28 min 30 sec** (CONFIRMED, Wikipedia; TouringPlans rounds to 29 min). **OPERATING.**

### Exhibits / galleries / walkthroughs
- **American Heritage Gallery** - current exhibition **"Creating Tradition: Innovation and Change in American Indian Art"**: authentic historical Native artifacts shown alongside contemporary American Indian art from seven US geographic regions, with interactive displays and artist narration (artists include fashion designer Loren Aragon and doll-maker Glenda McKay). **OPEN** (CONFIRMED, official; hours 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM).

### Shops
Permanent indoor: **1**
- Art of Disney - indoor, in the Heritage Manor building (CONFIRMED, official). Replaced **Heritage Manor Gifts**, which closed October 2019 and reopened as Art of Disney in December 2019; the merchandise shifted from historical books, presidential memorabilia and folk crafts to Disney artwork and collectibles. Heritage Manor Gifts should NOT be listed as a current shop.

Outdoor carts / kiosks: **0 confirmed**
- Some older listings place a "Camera Center" here; not confirmed on the current official directory.

### Kids activities
- **Kidcot Fun Stop** - American Adventure (CONFIRMED, official)

### Note
America Gardens Theatre, the outdoor lagoon-side amphitheater, sits in this pavilion but hosts live entertainment only (Garden Rocks, Eat to the Beat, Candlelight Processional, Disney on Broadway) - excluded per scope.

## 9. FRANCE

### Attractions (rides)
- **Remy's Ratatouille Adventure** - trackless dark ride using local positioning system (LPS) technology, in the pavilion's rear expansion - **4 min 40 sec** (CONFIRMED, Wikipedia). Official Disney page states no duration. Operating.
  - Recent change: brief refurbishment **10-14 November 2025**, during which the visual display was changed from three-dimensional to two-dimensional imagery.

### Films / theater shows
Both films run in the **same** theater, the **Palais du Cinéma**, on an **alternating daily schedule** - a guest cannot see both back to back in the middle of the day:
- **Impressions de France** - widescreen panoramic film (five-screen, 200-degree) - **18 min** (CONFIRMED: official Disney page and TouringPlans agree). Schedule: plays first thing in the morning, with the last morning show starting about 30 minutes after park opening, then again for roughly the last 90 minutes the park is open.
- **Beauty and the Beast Sing-Along** - **15 min** (CONFIRMED, official; TouringPlans agrees). Schedule: plays in the same theater through the middle of the day.

### Exhibits / galleries / walkthroughs
- None.

### Shops
Permanent indoor: **1 confirmed official, 5 per secondary sources**
- Plume et Palette - indoor, health and beauty, incl. Guerlain perfume (CONFIRMED, official)
- La Signature - indoor (UNCONFIRMED; secondary sources only)
- L'Esprit de la Provence - indoor (UNCONFIRMED; secondary sources only)
- Souvenirs de France - indoor (UNCONFIRMED; secondary sources only)
- Les Vins de France - indoor wine shop (UNCONFIRMED; secondary sources only)

Outdoor carts / kiosks: **0 retail confirmed**
- A caricature artist stand operates outdoors; it is a service kiosk, not a merchandise cart.

### Kids activities
- **Kidcot Fun Stop** - France (CONFIRMED, official)

### Shared with a neighbour
The France pavilion abuts the **International Gateway** park entrance; the World Traveler shop at that entrance is commonly grouped with France but is an entrance shop, not a France pavilion shop. Excluded from the France count.

## 11. CANADA

### Attractions (rides)
- None.

### Films / theater shows
- **Canada Far and Wide in Circle-Vision 360** - Circle-Vision 360 degree film, debuted 17 January 2020, succeeding "O Canada!" - **runtime DISPUTED: official Disney page describes it as "a newly updated edition of the classic, 12-minute Epcot Circle-Vision 360 presentation" (implying 12 min); TouringPlans lists 15 min.** Wikipedia's Canada pavilion article gives no runtime. Treat as 12-15 min, unresolved. Operating, no closure notice.

### Exhibits / galleries / walkthroughs
- None indoor. The pavilion's non-ride features are outdoor scenic: **Victoria Gardens** (inspired by Butchart Gardens near Victoria, BC, and the largest and most labour-intensive garden of any pavilion), the **Hôtel du Canada** facade, a rocky canyon with waterfall and reflecting pool, and **three totem poles** (two fiberglass originals replaced in 2017, plus one carved wooden pole from 1998). None of these is a gallery or walkthrough exhibit.

## SHARED / CROSS-PAVILION ITEMS

- **Kidcot Fun Stops** - one station in **every one of the 11** country pavilions, officially confirmed ("You'll find a Kidcot Fun Stop in every international pavilion at EPCOT"). Children color and collect a card at each pavilion; collecting all 11 earns a Mickey Mouse postcard. Official hours 10:00 AM - 9:00 PM. Sponsored by Ziploc. No age or height restriction. This is the single largest kids offering and it is shared across all eleven pavilions.

- **DuckTales World Showcase Adventure** - interactive scavenger-hunt game spanning multiple World Showcase pavilions (2022-present). **CURRENTLY CLOSED for refurbishment as of September 2026.** The Play Disney Parks app that ran it was **retired 16 September 2026** after an eight-year run, ending the existing version. Disney has confirmed the experience **will return in a modified format**, with details to come; a MagicBand+ based reimplementation is speculated but unconfirmed. Do not count this as an available offering right now.

- **Friendship Boats** - lagoon water transport linking World Showcase sides, approx. 10 min crossing (TouringPlans). Transport, not an attraction.

- **Village Traders** - kiosk-style shop at the African Outpost, physically located between the China and Germany pavilions. It belongs to **neither** pavilion and is excluded from both counts. AllEars described it in 2023 as mostly closed with a kiosk operating.

- **Luminous The Symphony of Us** - nighttime lagoon spectacular. Entertainment, excluded per scope.
